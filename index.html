The Riemann Hypothesis
over Finite FieldsEight short films, one big theorem

Solve y² = x³ − x + 1 in clock arithmetic mod 7 and you find 12 solutions. Why does that count always land within 2√p of p + 1? The answer runs through a donut, and the fully general version took until 1974, when Pierre Deligne proved it. This course walks the whole road, one picture at a time.
