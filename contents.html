Eight steps to |α| = √q

What did Grothendieck and Deligne actually do? This series builds up to the proof of the Weil conjectures, one idea at a time. It starts with why an elliptic curve is a torus, moves through homology, cohomology and counting points mod p, and ends with étale cohomology and Deligne's 1974 proof. Each video runs five to eleven minutes, with narration and captions.
